Quantitative Analysis

Leveraging advanced statistical and mathematical analysis to identify historical edge, measure asset relationships, and uncover structural market insights.

The core philosophy of Amos Brown Quantitative Research hinges on objective, math-based evaluation rather than narrative-driven speculation. By applying rigorous scientific methods to vast datasets, we are able to isolate variables that genuinely influence price discovery and discard market noise.

Analytical Focus Areas

Our quantitative engine searches for persistent, statistically significant behaviors that operate across various time horizons. This allows us to craft structural frameworks rather than fragile, single-use trading hypotheses.

  • Statistical Modeling: Applying robust regression architectures to model asset behavior under varying regimes.
  • Macro & Micro Indicators: Synthesizing high-level economic shifts with granular market indicators for a multi-layered market view.
  • Trend & Pattern Detection: Algorithmically identifying recurring behavioral structures that exhibit high persistence.
  • Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis: Evaluating how systems respond dynamically to shifts in input variables and macroeconomic shocks.
