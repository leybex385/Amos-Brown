Market Structure Research

In-depth study of structural liquidity, order flow behavior, and institutional participant dynamics underpinning the financial exchanges.

Prices do not simply move based on fundamental news; they are transacted through complex, multi-tiered algorithmic structures. Amos Brown’s research methodology places substantial emphasis on understanding the literal mechanics of the market. By mapping liquidity voids and analyzing order flow densities, we evaluate how capital actually changes hands.

Structural Dynamics

Our approach bypasses theoretical valuations and instead analyzes the hard realities of execution, order books, and participant friction.

  • Liquidity Analysis: Mapping how deep and accessible markets are across varying timeframes and stress conditions.
  • Market Microstructure: Studying the direct protocols, latencies, and mechanics of exchanges down to the tick level.
  • Order Flow Dynamics: Tracking aggregate shifting aggression between buyers and sellers to gauge authentic directional commitment.
  • Volume & Depth Analysis: Utilizing cumulative volume profiles and order book depth to establish structural inflection points.
